Original author: Randy Gill (2020)


The evil wizard “Balkor the Blue” has returned — to again threaten the peaceful land of Wasd!

He has sealed himself off at the top of the tower on Eastern Island, protected by one of his undead minions. There the sorcerer is preparing a powerful spell, which when completed will raise hordes of undead creatures, and surely spell doom for all life in the land!

There is only one hope for defeating Balkor the Blue. Centuries ago the magical Dagger of Venn was forged using the combined powers of the nine most powerful good wizards in the realm, in case the evil wizard should ever return. Alas, the dagger was hidden away in a long-since forgotten location.

Your quest is to find this magic dagger, and use it to destroy the Blue Wizard once and for all, before he deploys his deadly spell!

Time is running out!

The peaceful land of WASD is counting on you!
